The owner's Black Friday checklist

Keep January full before you buy anything. The smartest Black Friday move for a freelancer is not a purchase — it is a pipeline. Short, deadline-shaped projects get posted in waves this week, and the freelancers who pitch early start January with work already booked.

Paper before work, even on a deal. A discounted project is still a project: dates and next steps in the tracker, agreement out for signature before the first hour. Same-day signing is what turns a holiday yes into predictable income.

Sell the evenings you are not using. Online tutoring is booked in advance and paid on a schedule — a second stream that fills the gaps between client projects without any chasing.

Be findable when the rush ends. When holiday projects finish, the freelancers who keep getting found in search are the ones whose January does not depend on referrals alone.
